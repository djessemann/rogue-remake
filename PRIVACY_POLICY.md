# Privacy Policy for Roguetype

**Last Updated:** January 28, 2026

## Overview

Roguetype ("the App") is developed by Jesse Mann. This privacy policy explains how the App handles user information.

## Data Collection

**The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

Specifically, the App:
- Does not require user registration or accounts
- Does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
- Does not access device contacts, photos, or other personal files
- Does not track location data
- Does not use cookies or tracking technologies
- Does not contain advertisements
- Does not integrate with social media platforms

## Local Storage

The App may store game progress (such as high scores or save states) locally on your device using standard browser/app storage mechanisms. This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any servers
- Can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing app data

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. The App is suitable for all ages and contains no mechanisms for data collection from any users.
